== Detectors Implemented ==

T12: Absorption Detectors
  ABS-01 (Classic Absorption):
    - Scans bar levels for volume > AbsorptionVolumeMultiplier × avgLevelVol
    - Gate: bar range ≤ 0.5 points (2 ticks for NQ)
    - Direction: BidVol dominant → Long (sellers absorbed), AskVol → Short
    - Strength: min(1.0, vol / (multiplier * 2 * avg))
    - Tests: 3 (fire, no-fire normal volume, no-fire wide range)

  ABS-02 (Passive Absorption):
    - Looks back 3 bars: cumulative |delta| > 3 × avgBarDelta AND price range < 0.75
    - Direction: opposite to delta direction
    - Strength: min(1.0, |cumDelta| / (avgBarDelta * 5.0))
    - Tests: 3 (fire, no-fire too few bars, no-fire wide price range)

T13: Exhaustion Detectors
  EXH-01 (Exhaustion Print):
    - Finds highest-volume level at bar High or Low
    - Gate: volume > 2 × avgLevelVol AND close > 50% from extreme AND DeltaQualityScalar > 0.5
    - Direction: volume at High + close below midpoint → Short (buying exhaustion)
    - Strength: distanceFromExtreme / barRange
    - Tests: 4 (buying exhaustion at high, selling exhaustion at low, no-fire close near extreme, no-fire low delta quality)

  EXH-02 (Fading Momentum):
    - 3 bars: |delta[0]| < decay × |delta[1]| < decay × |delta[2]|
    - All deltas same sign, price still pushing same direction
    - Direction: opposite to fading direction
    - Strength: 1.0 - (|delta[0]| / |delta[2]|)
    - Tests: 4 (fading bullish, fading bearish, no-fire constant delta, no-fire sign change)

T14: Delta Detectors
  DELT-01 (Delta Divergence):
    - Uses MADDeltaPipeline.CheckDivergence(lookback=10)
    - Positive → bullish divergence → Long, Negative → bearish → Short
    - Strength: min(1.0, |divergence| / 500.0)
    - Gate: ≥ 10 bars history
    - Tests: 4 (bearish divergence, bullish divergence, no-fire agreement, no-fire insufficient history)

  DELT-02 (CVD Acceleration):
    - Uses MADDeltaPipeline.DeltaAccel and DeltaRoC
    - accel > 0 AND roc < 0 → Long (decelerating decline)
    - accel < 0 AND roc > 0 → Short (decelerating rise)
    - Strength: min(1.0, |accel| / 100.0)
    - Gate: ≥ 11 bars history, |accel| > 1.0
    - Tests: 4 (controlled pipeline state, acceleration reversal, no-fire insufficient history, no-fire same sign)
