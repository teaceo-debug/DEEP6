Bridge architecture:
- TcpListener on 127.0.0.1:9201 (separate from DataBridgeServer on 9200)
- Accepts ONE client (Python ML classification service)
- Background thread: AcceptLoop -> ReadLoop (auto-restarts on disconnect)
- Heartbeat: every 5s, {"type":"heartbeat","timestamp":<ms>}
- Outbound: wall_snapshot NDJSON per significant wall, sent during 30s prune cycle
- Inbound: wall_classification NDJSON parsed and applied to L2LevelStateV2
- Inbound: heartbeat_ack for IPC latency tracking

Thread safety:
- Bridge runs on dedicated background thread (IsBackground=true)
- _clientLock protects socket read/write/close
- _l2Lock used in ProcessInboundClassification for wall updates
- Lock ordering: _l2Lock -> _clientLock (no deadlock)
- volatile bool for IsConnected, _bridgeConnected

Lifecycle:
- Start: State.Realtime in OnStateChange
- Stop: State.Terminated in OnStateChange (bridge.Dispose())

NDJSON protocol:
- Outbound: {"type":"wall_snapshot","price":...,"side":"bid|ask","current_size":...,...}
- Inbound: {"type":"wall_classification","price":...,"side":"bid|ask","classification":"GENUINE|SPOOF|ICEBERG|STALE","confidence":0.92}
- Heartbeat: {"type":"heartbeat","timestamp":<unix_ms>}
- Heartbeat ack: {"type":"heartbeat_ack","echo_timestamp":<unix_ms>}
