Implementation Details:
  - OnMarketData: BBO tracking on Bid/Ask events (early return), trade classification
    using bestBid/bestAsk (aggressor 1=buy@ask, 2=sell@bid, 0=neutral), routes to _currentBar.AddTrade
  - OnMarketDepth: Fixed 50-slot arrays for bid/ask DOM levels, position-indexed updates,
    refill tracking (0→positive within 30s = iceberg signal)
  - MADDeltaPipeline: 500-entry circular buffer for CVD+close, CheckDivergence (price/CVD divergence),
    DeltaRoC (10-bar CVD rate of change), DeltaAccel (RoC acceleration)
  - FinalizeCurrentBar: finalizes bar, updates session CVD, feeds delta pipeline, caps at 500 bars
  - GetLiquidityWalls: returns prices where DOM volume exceeds threshold × average
  - GetDomImbalance: top-10 bid/ask volume ratio (1.0 = balanced)
  - GetRefillCount: refill count at price level for iceberg detection
